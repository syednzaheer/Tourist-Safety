🤖 AI Anomaly Detection

Runs your real recent location history through the backend's anomaly checks — three explainable rules plus a trained IsolationForest model, not a canned demo.

ℹ️ How it works:

Pulls your last several location pings (from map.html) and checks them for prolonged inactivity, implausible speed between pings, repeated risk-zone entries, and an overall movement-pattern score from a model trained on typical vs. unusual tourist movement. If you haven't pinged a location yet, register on form.html and ping a few points on the map first — otherwise there's no history for it to check.
